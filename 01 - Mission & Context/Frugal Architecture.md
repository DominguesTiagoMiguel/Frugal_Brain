Part of [[Data Efficiency Pillar]]. **This sub-pillar is the namesake of the vault/repo (Frugal_Brain).**

## Scope
- Define principles for evaluating complexity versus cost/value.
- Monitor patterns of oversizing.
- Promote architectural simplification.
- Define criteria for architectural rightsizing.
- Central question: *"Is the way we are building and consuming the most appropriate?"*
