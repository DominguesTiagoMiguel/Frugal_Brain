# CoE Data Architecture & Engineering — Mission

## Mission Statement
Define, govern, optimize and scale corporate data capabilities, ensuring that data solutions are secure, efficient, reusable and aligned with digital strategy and organization goals.

## Why this matters for the Brain
This mission is the root context for everything in this vault. Every agent ([[Atlas]], [[Curator]], [[Treasurer]], [[Maestro]]) and every topic note should trace back to at least one of these outcomes: **secure, efficient, reusable, aligned**.
