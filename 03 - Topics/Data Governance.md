# Data Governance

## Objectives
- Ownership definition.
- Data product accountability.
- Workspace governance.
- Metadata quality.
- Policy compliance.

## Key Concerns
- Ownership ambiguity.
- CMDB quality dependency.
- Policy gaming.
- Cost drift.
- Lack of workload classification.

## Recommended Capabilities
- Automated policy enforcement.
- Capacity assignment rules.
- Lineage tracking.
- Data catalog integration.
- Real-time governance monitoring.
