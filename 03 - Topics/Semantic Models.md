# Semantic Models

## Preferred Approach
Central semantic models supporting multiple reports.

## Benefits
- Measure reuse.
- Consistent business logic.
- Reduced duplication.
- Easier governance.

## Design Principles
- Shared certified datasets.
- Reusable KPIs.
- Clear ownership.
- Controlled lifecycle management.
